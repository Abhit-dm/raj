# RajWorthman v0.0.1 — Sprint 1 preview

*Drive | Manage | Grow*

This is the first preview of the RajWorthman driver partner app. Drivers sign up and submit their documents from a phone or computer. Your team reviews and approves them in the admin panel.

## What's included

**For drivers (Android app and website)**
- Sign up in 3 steps: personal details and photo, driving experience and licence, documents and vehicle.
- Take document photos with the camera or pick them from the gallery. Photos are compressed automatically.
- A dashboard showing application status, profile completion, documents, vehicles and notifications.
- Profile, documents, vehicles, notifications and settings (change password) pages.
- A notification when an application is approved or rejected.

**For your team (admin panel, website)**
- A list of all driver applications, with search and a status filter.
- Each driver's full details, documents and vehicles.
- Approve, reject, or mark under review.

## How to try it

**Android phone**
1. Install `RajWorthman-v0.0.1.apk`. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps".
2. Connect the phone to the **same Wi-Fi as the RajWorthman server PC**. This is required for this preview.
3. Open **RajWorthman** and tap **Create a New Driver Account**.

**Computer (any browser on the same Wi-Fi)**
- Driver site: `http://192.168.0.112:5173`
- Admin panel: `http://192.168.0.112:5173/admin/login` (admin login details are shared separately)

## Please note

This is a **preview for feedback**, not the final release:
- The app only works on the same Wi-Fi as the server. Access from anywhere comes once the server is hosted online.
- It is a test build, not yet on the Play Store or App Store.
- Trip History, Earnings and Support are placeholders for upcoming sprints.
